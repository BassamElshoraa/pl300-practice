PL-300 Offline

Open Open-PL300.cmd to start the local website in Edge.
Requires Node.js 22 or newer installed once on the computer.
Questions, figures, fonts, PDFs, bookmarks and study progress work without Internet.
Live AI and centrally managed student exams require Internet and their external services.
The built-in guidance option remains available without Internet. It is not live AI.
Local browser progress is separate from the online site. Use Backup & Restore to transfer it.
Keep the whole folder together. Do not open site/index.html directly.
